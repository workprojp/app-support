Privacy policy

Your practice footage is processed on your device.
Here is how the app and support service handle information.

Effective and last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy applies to Ugoki Replay (うごきリプレイ) and its support service.

1. Video, audio and pose information

Camera footage is processed on your device for delayed display, review and clip creation. Microphone audio is used when you choose to save a clip with audio. Body-pose and wrist/ankle trail estimation also run on your device. We do not upload this video, audio, pose or trail information to the developer’s server.

Temporary footage that you do not save is deleted when practice ends. Saved clips remain in the app. If you export to Photos or share a clip, the selected destination handles that copy.

2. Permissions

Camera access is used to capture and review practice. Optional microphone access adds surrounding sound to saved clips. Optional photo-add access exports a selected clip to your photo library.

You can change permissions in device settings. Features that need a permission will not work without it.

3. Settings, previews and purchases

The app stores settings and Pro preview usage on your device. Purchases and restoration use Apple StoreKit; the app processes transaction information to verify Pro access.

Apple handles payment. The developer does not receive your credit card number or other payment credentials. Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply to App Store purchases.

4. Ads, tracking and usage

The app has no ads, advertising tracking or external transmission for developer-operated usage analytics. No separate app account is required.

Depending on your settings and Apple’s policies, Apple may process diagnostics or App Store usage information and provide reports to developers. This does not upload your practice videos to us.

5. Retention, deletion and backups

You can delete saved clips inside the app. Deleting the app also removes its local app data from the device. Copies exported to Photos or shared elsewhere, and device backups, are managed separately and must be deleted at those destinations if needed.

Saved clips and settings may be included in device or iCloud backups, depending on your backup settings. The app does not provide its own cross-device clip sync.

6. Support inquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if included, your message and any optional attachments. We use this information to respond, investigate problems and provide support. We use Gmail, so support emails are processed and stored through Google’s services.

We keep support information for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and maintain necessary records, then delete information that is no longer needed. Contact us below to request deletion or discuss your information. Some records may need to be retained for investigations or legal obligations.

7. Sharing information

We do not sell support information or share it for advertising. We do not disclose it to third parties except with your consent, through service providers needed to handle support, or as required by law.

8. This website

This support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add our own analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. GitHub may process IP addresses and other access information to deliver the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Changes and contact

We update this page when features or information practices change. Where appropriate, we will also communicate important changes in the app or through another suitable channel.

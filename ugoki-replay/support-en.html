A little help when you need it.

A delay camera for dance practice and sports form review.
Find answers about using the app, Pro and saved clips.

For iPhone and iPad

Start in three steps.

  1. Place your device somewhere stable and allow camera access.
  2. Choose a delay and start practice. You will see live video while the buffer fills.
  3. Move, then check the screen. Pause to review a moment more closely.

Camera delays up to 20 seconds are free. Keep your practice area safe and respect the privacy of anyone in view.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the camera not showing video?

Check the camera permission for Ugoki Replay in your device settings. Close other camera apps and restart the app.

Why does the delayed view not start immediately?

The app shows live video while it builds up enough footage for your selected delay. Keep your device in position and wait for the buffer to fill.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

Free features include 5/10/15/20-second delays, front/rear camera switching, camera mirroring, and playback, sharing and deletion of existing clips. Saved clips support 1–2x playback, fullscreen and looping. Pro adds 30/60-second delays, slow motion down to 0.25x, frame stepping, pose and trails, creation of 1–30-second clips, export to Photos and saved-clip mirroring. Both plans retain the most recent three minutes of practice footage during a session.

Will a Pro preview charge me automatically?

No. You can manually start up to three five-minute Pro previews. They never turn into a paid purchase automatically. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no monthly fee or automatic renewal. Your local price is shown in the app before purchase.

How do I restore Pro?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. Choose Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen. If it does not appear, check your connection and restart the app.

Why do pose lines or trails look wrong?

Use good lighting and keep one person’s full body in view. Pose and trails are estimates and may be affected by fast movement, obstructions or poor visibility. They are not technique scores, medical assessments or precise measurements.

Why is a saved clip silent?

A clip must have been saved with audio enabled. Audio plays at 1x with sound turned on. Slow motion, faster playback and the delayed camera view are silent.

Where are my clips stored?

Created clips stay inside the app. Export to Photos requires Pro. Sharing an existing clip is free. There is no built-in sync between devices. Export or share anything you want to keep before deleting the app.

Why can’t I save to Photos?

Check your available storage, Pro access and photo-add permission in device settings. Permission is requested when you export a clip.

How do I request a refund?

App Store refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to make a request. Apple determines eligibility.
